3) FI Documents & Line Items

BKPF Accounting document header: PK (MANDT, BUKRS, GJAHR, BELNR); keys: BLART, BUDAT, WAERS, source link AWTYP/AWKEY

BSEG Line items: PK (MANDT, BUKRS, GJAHR, BELNR, BUZEI); keys: HKONT (GL), KUNNR, LIFNR, DMBTR, WRBTR

FAGLFLEXA New GL line-item/ledger view (doc-based); typically joins back to BKPF/BSEG via (RBUKRS=BUKRS, GJAHR, BELNR [, DOCLN↔BUZEI]) where populated.

Open/Cleared items (derived from BSEG)

BSID / BSAD Customer open/cleared: (BUKRS, KUNNR, GJAHR, BELNR, BUZEI)

BSIK / BSAK Vendor open/cleared: (BUKRS, LIFNR, GJAHR, BELNR, BUZEI)

Joins

-- Header → line items
select h.bukrs, h.gjahr, h.belnr, h.blart, i.buzei, i.hkont, i.kunnr, i.lifnr, i.dmbtr
from bkpf h
join bseg i
  on i.mandt=h.mandt and i.bukrs=h.bukrs and i.gjahr=h.gjahr and i.belnr=h.belnr
where h.mandt=:mandt;

-- Open items (customer)
select b.*
from bsid b
join bkpf h
  on h.mandt=b.mandt and h.bukrs=b.bukrs and h.gjahr=b.gjahr and h.belnr=b.belnr
where b.mandt=:mandt;

5) CO: Cost & Profit Centers

CSKS Cost center master: KOKRS (controlling area), KOSTL, DATBI/DATAB (validity); text in CSKT: KOKRS, KOSTL, SPRAS, DATBI

CEPC Profit center: PRCTR, KOKRS; text in CEPCT: PRCTR, KOKRS, SPRAS

Joins

-- Cost center with text (as-of date)
select k.kokrs, k.kostl, t.ltext
from csks k
left join cskt t
  on t.mandt=k.mandt and t.kokrs=k.kokrs and t.kostl=k.kostl and t.spras=:spras
where k.mandt=:mandt
  and :date between k.datab and k.datbi;

7) SD Billing (links back to FI)

VBRK Billing header: VBELN (billing doc), FKART, KUNNR, VKORG, FKDAT (billing date), WAERK

VBRP Billing item: VBELN, POSNR, MATNR, FKIMG, NETWR

TVFK/TVFKT Billing type + text: FKART (+ SPRAS)

TVKO Sales org: VKORG

TVTWT (Tax classification text; varies by config)

Accounting link (common pattern)

From FI back to SD: BKPF.AWTYP='VBRK' and BKPF.AWKEY=VBRK-VBELN (or use the accounting document number stored via SD→FI interface if present).

Joins

-- Billing ↔ accounting document via AWTYP/AWKEY
select b.vbeln, b.fkart, k.bukrs, k.belnr, k.gjahr
from vbrk b
join bkpf k
  on k.mandt=b.mandt
 and k.awtyp='VBRK'
 and k.awkey=b.vbeln   -- sometimes padded/concatenated; check client-specific AWKEY format
where b.mandt=:mandt;

8) Master/Customizing “glue” (texts & groups)

T077K / T077Y / T077X Account-group customizing & texts (commonly customer/vendor groups); join from KNA1.KTOKD or LFA1.KTOKK to the respective table key; also SPRAS for text variants.

T008T Text for allocation keys/reason codes (depends on use); join by its key + SPRAS.

TVFKT, T023T, CSKT, CEPCT, SKAT — all are text tables (…T) and join to their base by base-key + SPRAS.

9) Frequently-used Join Recipes (copy/paste)

A) FI document → GL account + GL text

select i.bukrs, i.gjahr, i.belnr, i.buzei, i.hkont, t.txt50 as gl_text
from bseg i
join bkpf h
  on h.mandt=i.mandt and h.bukrs=i.bukrs and h.gjahr=i.gjahr and h.belnr=i.belnr
join t001 c
  on c.mandt=i.mandt and c.bukrs=i.bukrs
join ska1 g
  on g.mandt=i.mandt and g.ktopl=c.ktopl and g.saknr=i.hkont
left join skat t
  on t.mandt=g.mandt and t.ktopl=g.ktopl and t.saknr=g.saknr and t.spras=:spras
where i.mandt=:mandt;

10) Gotchas & best practices

Client field: Always filter MANDT to avoid cross-client noise.

AWKEY format: It can be concatenated/padded; test with a few known documents before relying on it for SD/MM→FI back-links.

Validity intervals: CO objects (CSKS/CSKT) and some texts have DATAB/DATBI; join with an “as-of date”.

Chart of accounts: To resolve GL texts correctly, derive KTOPL from T001 for the company code and join via (KTOPL, SAKNR).

Open/cleared item tables (BS) are snapshots** derived from BSEG. If completeness matters, reconcile with BSEG.

IDs with leading zeros: Ensure your ETL preserves them (use string types).

Possible typos in your list (verify in your system):

KNW → likely KNVV (Customer Sales Area).

(SKS → likely CSKS (Cost center master).

TOURT → likely TCURT (Currency text).
